## Elapsed math

**How does the time shown stay correct while the wall clock keeps moving during a pause?**

A pause must not add to the time shown. Elapsed is subtracting clock readings, not counting frames.

<ElapsedMath />

Running several stopwatches at once, or changing how often the UI updates? That is scheduling rather than math — see [Data flow while running](/docs/architecture#data-flow-while-running).
